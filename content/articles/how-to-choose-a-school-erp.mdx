## The Strategic Importance of ERP Selection

Selecting a School ERP is a multi-year institutional commitment. A well-chosen system boosts parent satisfaction, cuts clerical overhead by 40%, and prevents revenue leakage. A poor choice leads to staff frustration, abandoned software licenses, and expensive data recovery projects.

Before signing a contract with any software vendor, school boards and IT committees should assess prospective systems against this six-pillar evaluation framework.

---

## 6-Pillar School ERP Evaluation Framework

```
+-------------------------------------------------------------+
|               ERP Evaluation Criteria Matrix                |
+-------------------------------------------------------------+
 1. Core Usability       -> Can teachers take roll in < 30 seconds?
 2. Mobile Accessibility -> Is there a fast, native/PWA parent app?
 3. Financial Robustness -> Does fee collection sync with general ledger?
 4. Data Privacy/Backups -> Is data encrypted at rest and backed up daily?
 5. Scalability          -> Can it handle enrollment growth seamlessly?
 6. Vendor Support       -> What are the guaranteed SLA response times?
```

### Pillar 1: Usability and Adoption Speed
If a system is unintuitive, teachers will resist using it. During product demonstrations, demand a live test:
- Can a non-technical teacher log attendance for 35 students in under one minute?
- Can exam marks be entered quickly using keyboard tab navigation without mouse hunting?

### Pillar 2: Native Mobile Accessibility for Parents
Parents rarely log into desktop computer browsers to check school circulars. The ERP must provide either an intuitive mobile app or a responsive progressive web application (PWA) with push notification capabilities.

### Pillar 3: Financial Integrity & Local Compliance
Ensure the fee management module supports:
- Multi-tier fee slabs (tuition, transport zones, extracurriculars)
- Automated discount and scholarship rules
- Local banking and mobile wallet integration (e.g., eSewa/Khalti for Nepal, or Stripe/PayPal internationally)
- Double-entry accounting synchronization

### Pillar 4: Security, Privacy & Data Ownership
Student demographic records, family financial status, and health notes require stringent protection:
- Does the vendor contract guarantee that the school retains 100% legal ownership of its data?
- Can the school export its complete database in standard SQL/CSV format at any time without punitive fees?
- Are user sessions protected by role-based access control (RBAC)?

### Pillar 5: Implementation Timeline & Staff Training
Ask the vendor for their detailed rollout schedule:
- Is on-site or interactive virtual staff training included in the purchase price?
- Does the vendor provide dedicated data migration specialists to import past student records?

### Pillar 6: Transparent Pricing Model
Beware of hidden costs:
- Is pricing per student, per user, or a flat annual license?
- Are SMS gateway credits billed separately?
- What are the recurring annual maintenance and server hosting fees?

## Conclusion

Never rush an ERP purchase. Form a committee comprising academic coordinators, bursars, and IT leads. Run a pilot test with actual school data, and verify references from existing client institutions before finalizing your implementation.
